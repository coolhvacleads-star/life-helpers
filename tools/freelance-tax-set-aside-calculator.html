How to use a tax set-aside for freelance income

Many freelancers move a fixed percentage of every payment into a separate savings account so the money is there when taxes are due. This calculator only does the math at the percentage you enter. It does not know your tax rate, so pick the percentage with a tax professional or check the IRS estimated tax page linked above.

A simple set-aside routine

  • Each time a client pays, run the numbers and move the set-aside the same day.
  • Track income, expenses and mileage in one place so the year-end total is easy.
