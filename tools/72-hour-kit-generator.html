How much water for a 72-hour kit?

Ready.gov recommends at least one gallon of water per person per day, for several days. A family of four needs at least 12 gallons for three days. Pets need water too. Ask your vet how much.

What goes in a basic kit

Water, food that does not spoil, a battery or hand-crank radio, flashlight, first aid kit, extra batteries, a whistle, dust masks, a manual can opener, local maps and phone chargers. Then add what your own household needs: medicine, baby supplies, pet food and backup power for medical devices.
