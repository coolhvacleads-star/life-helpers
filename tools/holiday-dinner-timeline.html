When should I start thawing the turkey?

USDA says to allow about 24 hours in the refrigerator for every 4 to 5 pounds. This tool uses 4 pounds per day so you have a little extra time. A 16 lb turkey needs about 4 days, so for a Thursday dinner, move it to the fridge by Sunday.

Building the dinner-day schedule

Start from the time you want to eat. Take away the resting time, then the roasting time from your recipe. That is when the turkey goes in. Sides that share the oven go in while the turkey rests.
