What goes on a construction change order

A clear change order usually describes the added or changed work, lists labor and materials, shows markup and tax, states the new contract total and any schedule change, and gets the client's signature before the work starts. Your contract and your state's rules decide what a valid change order needs.

When to write one

  • Any "while you're here, could you also…" request.
  • Material price changes or hidden conditions found mid-job.
  • Upgraded selections that go over an allowance.
