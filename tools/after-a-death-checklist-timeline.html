After a death: a gentle checklist and timeline for executors and families

I'm sorry for your loss. Enter the date of death and you'll get a step-by-step list for the first days through the first year, with suggested dates. Take it one step at a time.

The dates below are a suggested pace to help you organize, not legal deadlines. Skip anything that doesn't apply. Your date and ticks are saved only in this browser.

Not legal, tax or financial advice. This is an organizing checklist. Probate rules, deadlines and forms are different in every state and sometimes every county, so confirm anything that matters with your county probate court (find it at usa.gov/courts), an attorney, a tax professional, or the agency involved.

What to do in the first 90 days after a death

The first days are about the funeral, death certificates and telling family. The following weeks are about notifying Social Security, banks, insurers and creditors, and finding out from your county probate court or an attorney whether probate is needed. Keep a log of every call and every receipt. This page is an organizing checklist, not legal advice.
