How the debt snowball and debt avalanche methods differ

Both methods pay the minimum on every debt and put every extra dollar on one target debt. When that debt is gone, its payment rolls into the next one. Snowball targets the smallest balance first, so you get early wins. Avalanche targets the highest interest rate first, which usually costs less interest. The calculator runs both month by month so you can pick the one you will stick with.

Debt payoff calculator tips

  • Use the balance, APR and minimum from your latest statements.
  • Try a few different extra amounts to see how your payoff date moves.
  • Print the result and keep it where you will see it.
