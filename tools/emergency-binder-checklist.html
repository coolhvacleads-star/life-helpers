How to start an in case of emergency binder

Start with people to call and medical information, since those are needed first. Then record where each important document is kept rather than copying sensitive numbers. Review it every 6 to 12 months and tell one trusted person where it is.

Good times to update it

  • Before holiday travel or a long trip.
  • After a move, a new job, a new baby or a new insurance policy.
