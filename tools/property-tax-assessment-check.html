How to tell if your assessment is too high

Find 3 to 5 homes like yours that sold close to the valuation date on your notice. Divide each sale price by its square footage, take the middle (median) number, and multiply by your square footage. If that comes out well below your notice value, it may be worth asking your assessor for a review.

Check your property record too

Errors are common and often the easiest fix: wrong square footage, an extra bathroom, a finished basement you don't have. Ask your assessor for your property record card.
