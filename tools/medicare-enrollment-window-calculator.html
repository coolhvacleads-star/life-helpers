How the 7-month window works

Your Initial Enrollment Period starts 3 months before the month you turn 65, includes that month, and ends 3 months after it. If your birthday is on the 1st, Medicare counts the month before as the month you turn 65.

When does Part B start?

Sign up before the month you turn 65 and coverage starts that month. Sign up in the month you turn 65 or the 3 months after, and coverage starts the month after you sign up. Missing the window can mean a late penalty that lasts as long as you have Part B, unless you qualify for a Special Enrollment Period.
