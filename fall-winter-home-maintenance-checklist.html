Questions

When should I do fall home maintenance?

Before your first hard freeze, so the hoses are drained and the furnace has been checked before you need it. Clean the gutters once most of the leaves are down. Exact timing depends on where you live.

Should I do furnace or chimney work myself?

Changing a filter is a homeowner job. For gas, electrical, roof, chimney or structural work, hire a licensed professional and follow the manufacturer's instructions.
