Fantasy football last place punishment ideas (funny, safe and printable)

The best last place punishment is one the whole league laughs about and the loser can actually finish. Here's how to set it up so it sticks, plus 10 ideas that cost nothing.

Set the rules before the draft

Most punishment fights happen because the league picks the punishment after someone is already losing. Settle these five things at the draft and write them down:

10 last place punishments that cost nothing

  1. Change your profile picture to a photo the league picks, for one week.
  2. Write a 300-word apology poem and read it at the draft.
  3. Draft last in round one next season.
  4. Take the league's worst team name next season (the league votes).
  5. Narrate a game like a nature documentary, on video.
  6. Make a slideshow titled "What Went Wrong" and present it.
  7. Answer the group chat only in rhyme for 48 hours.
  8. Give the champion a handwritten thank-you note.
  9. Do a pregame speech for the champion, on video.
  10. Write the season recap newsletter, with a compliment for every team but yours.

These come from the 20-idea list in our League Pack. All of them work for long-distance leagues, because the proof goes in the group chat.

Long-distance league? Make it a group chat punishment

If your league never gets together in person, pick a punishment that happens in the chat, where everyone sees it. One option: the loser colors a joke coloring book about their own season and posts every finished page. The league votes on the worst page.

Questions commissioners ask

When should we decide the punishment?

Before the draft. A punishment picked in December looks like it targets one person, and the loser has the most reason to argue.

What if the loser refuses?

Agree on a backup before the season, like drafting last in round one or taking the league-voted team name.
