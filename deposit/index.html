Why landlords lose deposit disputes

Usually it's the paperwork, not the facts: no signed move-in report, no before photos, a statement sent after the deadline, or a deduction with no receipt behind it. State deposit laws often set strict deadlines and penalties, so getting the process right matters as much as the condition of the unit.

  1. Document move-in with a signed condition report and photos.
  2. Know your deadline from your state's official statute before the keys come back.
  3. Itemize every deduction with evidence, and prorate older items.
  4. Send the statement on time, in a way you can prove.
