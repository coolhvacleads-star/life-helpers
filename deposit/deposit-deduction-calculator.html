Security deposit deduction calculator with proration

List each deduction. For items that wear out over time (carpet, paint, blinds, appliances), enter the useful life and age to charge only the remaining value: cost × (life − age) ÷ life, never below $0. Leave life blank to use the full amount, for example for unpaid rent or a receipt for cleaning.

Not legal advice. Whether a deduction is allowed, and whether proration is expected, depends on your state's law and the facts. Normal wear and tear generally can't be deducted.

How to itemize security deposit deductions

List each item, attach a receipt or estimate, and note the before and after condition from your move-in and move-out reports. For items with a useful life, charge only the remaining value. Normal wear and tear generally can't be deducted.
